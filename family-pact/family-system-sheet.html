Digital Code

  1. 01Spotify and WhatsApp are unlimited in Kidslox for music and social planning only. Do not misuse them by watching videos on them.
  2. 02No looking at phones in bed, in morning or evening.
  3. 03No TikTok, YouTube Shorts or Reels in the first hour after waking or the final hour before bed.
  4. 04Phones charge downstairs overnight. No ad hoc top-ups when phone time has been used badly.
  5. 05Bought phone time must be bought in advance. One voucher per day max, and unused daily phone time does not normally roll over.

Nag-o-meter

  • Nagged once: Warning nag - Yellow card
  • Nagged twice: Minus 30 minutes phone time from the next day - Red card
  • Nagged three times: Minus additional 60 minutes phone time from the next day
  • Nagged four times: 20% reduction in next week allowance
  • Nagged five times: 40% reduction in next week allowance
  • Nagged six or more times: 50% reduction plus further sanctions
  • Appeals for review can be submitted in writing only
Every 5 house points can be exchanged for 30 minutes extra phone time or £2 allowance credit
